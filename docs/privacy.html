Scoretender

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

In short: Scoretender (called Änärivahti in Finland) doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and the developer receives no data from the app.

Who this policy is from

Scoretender is an Android app made by Lauri Nevala, an independent developer. This policy covers the app and this website.

Scores and stats come from the NHL

The app downloads schedules, scores, stats, team logos and player photos straight from the National Hockey League's public servers, such as api-web.nhle.com, api.nhle.com and assets.nhle.com. There is no server of the developer in between.

As with any website you visit, those servers see your device's IP address and basic technical details, such as the app's network library version. The app doesn't send any identifier, location or account information with these requests.

To highlight players from the country you pick, the app asks the NHL's stats server for that country's players, so that request includes the country's code (for example FIN).

The NHL runs these servers, not the developer. How the NHL handles requests is covered by the NHL's own privacy policy, available at nhl.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Your country choice. The country whose players you highlight is saved on your phone. The first time, the app suggests one based on your phone's region setting. That check happens on the phone and isn't sent anywhere.
  • Downloaded images. Team logos and player photos are kept in the app's cache, so they don't need to be downloaded again.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes both. If you use Android's backup, Android may include the country choice in your phone's backup, under Google's terms for that backup.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission: internet access, to download scores. It doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change. Earlier versions can be seen in the history of this page on GitHub.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome as an issue on GitHub.
