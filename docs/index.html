Scoretender

Every night's hockey scores at a glance, with the players from your country up front. Simple, fast, and free of ads and tracking.

Coming soon to Google Play Source on GitHub

Your players first

Pick a country, and its scorers are listed under every score with their goals and assists. Anyone else who has a three-point night gets a mention too.

Day by day

Swipe to yesterday's results or tomorrow's schedule, or jump to any date from the calendar. Each day shows how many games are on.

The whole game

Tap a game for the score by period, who scored, team stats, the goalies and the three stars.

  • Live games update on their own. The header shows how many are on right now.
  • Light and dark. Follows your phone's theme, with a themed icon on Android 13 and newer.
  • No account, ads or tracking. The privacy policy is short because there's little to say.

On Finnish phones

Änärivahti

/ˈænæriˌʋɑhti/ noun, Finnish

The app's name in Finland. From Änäri, what Finns call the NHL, and vahti, a watcher. Also, a goalie.
