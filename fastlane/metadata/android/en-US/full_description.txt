Scoretender shows last night's NHL results, tonight's schedule and live scores, with your country's scorers listed under every score.

• Your players first: pick a country and see how its players did in every game, with goals, assists and ice time. Anyone else with a three-point night shows up too.
• Day by day: swipe between days, or jump to any date from the calendar.
• Live by itself: games in progress update on their own.
• The whole game: score by period, every goal and who set it up, team stats, goalies and the three stars.
• Before the game: each team's top scorers over the last five games, and the goalies' season stats.
• Light and dark themes, and a themed icon on Android 13 and newer.

Players from 17 countries can be followed, including Canada, the United States, Sweden, Finland, Czechia, Switzerland, Slovakia and Germany.

No account, no ads and no tracking. The app doesn't collect any personal data.

Scoretender is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the National Hockey League. NHL and team names, logos and data belong to the NHL and its teams.
